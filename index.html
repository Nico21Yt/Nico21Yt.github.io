The Illusion of Specialization: Unveiling the Domain-Invariant “Standing Committee” in Mixture-of-Experts Models

ACL 2026 · co-first author with Yan Wang and Nanhan Shen, with Jinyan Su, Jimin Huang, and Zining Zhu

We introduce CommitteeAudit and show that routing in Mixture-of-Experts models is dominated by a domain-invariant “Standing Committee” of experts, challenging the common assumption of strong domain specialization.
